# Giese wave-pinning native reproduction snapshot v1

This snapshot is generated by `scripts/plot_giese_native.py` from the 14
production checkpoints created by `workflow/run_giese_native.slurm`.

It contains the seven-diameter single- and two-stimulus size metrics,
conservation and convergence summaries, representative membrane profiles, and
selected 2D bulk fields.  The approximate winner-selection targets overlaid in
the size figure (43 s at 3 µm and 953 s at 6 µm) are the acceptance values
recorded in `docs/jobs/01_giese.txt`; they are reference values, not simulated
data.

Raw per-time profiles, meshes, and field arrays remain under the ignored
`reports/output/giese_wp/` tree.

## Reproduction status

The solver passes its conservation and discretization checks and recovers no
persistent polarization at 1.5 µm plus persistent single-site polarization at
all sampled diameters from 3--15 µm.  The supplied approximate competition-time
acceptance values were **not** recovered: the 3-µm winner-selection time is
131 s rather than about 43 s, and two sites remain at 4.5 µm and above through
2000 s (the target expects competition through 6 µm).  The strict published PF
definition also reaches 90% of its maximum at the first 1-s output because the
stimulus immediately creates a narrow above-mean component.  These discrepancies
are retained in the snapshot rather than relabelled as a successful Figure 4
reproduction.

## Follow-up diagnostics

`fix_diagnostics.csv` records the prioritized reproduction checks at 3 µm.
Using the conflicting half-mass PF changes the comparison but does not recover
43 s: the conservative regular-mesh run reaches 90% of its final half-mass PF
at 127 s.  The paper-style semi-implicit split gives 132 s and accumulates a
relative mass error of about 0.002 over 300 s.  Irregular Gmsh meshes refined at
the membrane give 129 s with 1,429 triangles and 130 s with 4,690 triangles.
Replacing the rounded initial value with the exact homogeneous equilibrium
gives the largest improvement, to 122 s, which is still far from the target.
