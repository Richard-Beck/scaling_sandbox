Standalone Hosny drift sensitivity analysis for the primary Gaussian OU hierarchy. Panels A–B show the local relative persistence/noise surface with WGD− persistence/noise fixed; Hosny signed drifts are either fixed at the selected fit or both reoptimized at every point. Broad-grid context appears in each inset. Lines show changes in scaled data misfit (A) or regularized total loss (B); contour color identifies Δ 1, 4, and 9, and solid pale versus dashed saturated lines identify fixed versus refitted drifts. The two drift treatments nearly overlap. Panel C profiles each signed drift and their contrast while reoptimizing the remaining parameters. Panel D shows Hosny-specific persistence/noise folds from selected sampled one-dimensional profiles within total Δ 9; all points use the same circle and color represents increase in total fit objective. A hollow circle marks the selected fit. Panels E–F compare study-specific folds and their pooled fold from the five-study fit, the same full-fit parameters summarized across the remaining four studies, and a refit excluding Hosny. The objective uses heuristic residual scales and regularization, is not calibrated to meaningful statistical or biological units, and is not a likelihood. Δ values are descriptive sensitivity guides, not confidence intervals; unions of sampled one-dimensional profiles do not form a joint region. Hosny path predictions use stable Rice calculations; a normal asymptotic fallback applies only in the extreme high drift-to-step-noise tail, as recorded in analysis provenance. Sampled Hosny τ/σ folds range from 11.2–12.4/0.155–0.180 at Δ≤1 to 9.7–12.6/0.122–0.199 at Δ≤9. Pooled τ/σ folds are 15.16/0.138 (five studies), 16.16/0.131 (same fit, four-study composition), and 18.66/0.116 (four-study refit).
