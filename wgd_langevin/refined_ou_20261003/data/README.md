# Frozen reviewed inputs

These tables update the five-study Gaussian OU manuscript hierarchy selected as `wgd_langevin/gaussian_hierarchy/results/fit_05.json`. They do not add the HIF/RhoA pairs from the separate persistence campaigns, pool context clones, or fit representative movie tracks. Source extracts remain immutable. Regenerate from repository root with `python -B wgd_langevin/refined_ou_20261003/data/prepare_inputs.py`.

- `observations.csv`: all 25 original scalar, context and diagnostic entries retained, with reviewed Xuan B/C/E, Mallin central bars, Jemaà 2017 and Jemaà 2023 clone means. D1/T1 and Cl1/Cl8 retain their original fitting roles. Context clones remain context. Xuan original late MSD targets are `replaced`, so they are not scored again. Early 600-second MSD checks use log-log interpolation of the reviewed traces and overlap the fitted curves; they are correlated checks, not independent holdouts. Crossover, slow fraction and slope retain their prior roles and unresolved thresholds/windows.
- `xuan_msd_curve.csv`: all 510 native trace points, with plotted lag in hours, digitized population MSD, source hash, pixel coordinates and raster uncertainty. Curves are means/population summaries, not representative movie tracks. Points are correlated raster samples, not independent cells. Each group's positive lag extent is split into three equal intervals in log lag, covering early, intermediate and late lags. Each interval contributes one third of that group's normalized squared residual, with total weight one for the entire curve, equal to one speed target. `objective_weight` is 1/(3 × number of points in its bin). `log_scale` preserves the original late-MSD descriptive half-log widths, approximately 0.202733 and 0.111572. Point `low/high` are raster bounds used for plotting/diagnosis, not residual weights.
- `hosny_observations.csv`: only original hypoxic baseline N/P. Each of five metrics has Q1, median, Q3 for each group. Fit path, directness and parallel FMI medians; reserve their quartiles plus endpoint and perpendicular FMI for prediction checks. IQR is biological spread used as the legacy descriptive residual scale (floor 0.04), never an error bar on the median. The source is the reviewed preprint; journal numerical equivalence remains unverified. Distances are 16-hour equivalents from the paper's variable-duration rescaling.
- `adapter.py`: imports frozen tables and exports `observations(study, checks=False)` and `curve_observations()`. The former preserves the original observation dictionary interface, including all Hosny quartiles; the fitter must keep its original median mask. The latter includes full-curve weights. It rejects unrequested study groups.
- `source_provenance.json`: source-table/disposition SHA-256 hashes, study scope and row counts. `prepare_inputs.py` checks curve count, Hosny count and normalized curve weights on regeneration.

Scalar `low/high` preserve each prior relative descriptive reading tolerance centered on the revised digitized value. They are heuristic fitting scales; tighter raster precision does not become stronger biological evidence. `raster_low/high`, reading and calibration allowances, and mean SEM amplitudes are retained separately. SEM is neither substituted for the heuristic fit tolerance nor mislabeled as a confidence interval. Jemaà 2023 unresolved SEM endpoints remain blank. Mallin's horizontal bars have an unspecified statistic; the existing model mean readout remains an explicit approximation and those values are never relabeled as measured means or medians. Full MSD curve widths likewise retain the existing descriptive scale rather than counting digitization precision as replicate uncertainty.

Movie acquisition timing, Hosny variable-duration rescaling, Jemaà 2017 20/24-hour discrepancy, and incomplete dots remain source limitations. Fine numeric precision supports exact regeneration, not biological precision. No new groups or population uncertainty estimates are inferred.

Validate the frozen mapping with `python -B wgd_langevin/refined_ou_20261003/data/validate_inputs.py`; `input_validation.json` records the successful scope, statistic, source-hash and curve-weight checks. Xuan extracted combined raster bounds are retained as such; separately tabulated reading and calibration components follow the source's 1.5-pixel glyph and 1-pixel axis allowances, while the source combined propagation uses their approximately 1.803-pixel quadrature allowance. This decomposition changes metadata only, never fitted values or descriptive scales.
