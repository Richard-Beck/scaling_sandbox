# Figure 3 caption

A, observed and primary-fit OU-predicted WGD+ / WGD− folds for nine migration summaries (the last shared population lag summarizes the Xuan MSD fold, with native observed/predicted traces interpolated to that shared lag); Hosny uses group medians. B, full digitized population MSD curves (solid) and fitted OU ensemble MSD (dashed), on logarithmic lag and MSD axes to expose the crossover. The open circle marks the population-curve crossover interpolated between native trace samples, approximately 2.32 hours; this is a descriptive digitized crossing, not a precise population estimate. C, enlarged representative primary-fit model-generated Xuan2018 tracks. D, with WGD− fixed, relative WGD+ persistence/noise data-misfit and regularized objective surfaces. E, paired primary-fit persistence and velocity-noise estimates. F, WGD+ / WGD− persistence folds across prior/pooling scenarios, including their pooled geometric mean. Track paths are simulations, not observed individual cells. Surface scores use the model's heuristic weighted objective; their scale is not a calibrated likelihood or biological unit. Prior/pooling contrasts are conditional sensitivity analyses, not uncertainty intervals.
