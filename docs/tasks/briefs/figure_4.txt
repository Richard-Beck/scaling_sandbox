# Figure 4 brief: experiments, analyses, supplement, decision criteria and figure construction

**Scope.** Four ring models: Wave-pinning, Goryachev, Holmes 3 and Spring. The existing campaign (`revision_noise_strength_replication_scoped_20261019`, plus the 26-case Goryachev refinement) is the starting point:

- Chemical circumferences 20, 40 and 60; Spring resting circumferences 1, 2 and 3.
- Lognormal input I = f·I_ref·exp(σZ − σ²/2) with σ = 1. Spatial kernel 1.5 (Spring 0.15); OU knot relaxation time 50 (Spring 5).
- Horizon 32,000 (Spring 3,200). The first half is discarded.
- Scalar cadence 5 (Spring 0.5); profile cadence 40 (Spring 4).
- Twelve seeds at f = 0.001–0.1; two seeds at the outer strengths.

Use physical arc throughout, including Spring's actual circumference.

## 2. Analyses

**A1. Heading persistence.**
- Compute heading autocorrelation C(lag) at full scalar cadence, from one cadence step up to half the analysis window. Use only pairs where both endpoints have R ≥ 0.1, and report usable-pair coverage alongside.
- Uncertainty: 2,000 whole-seed bootstrap resamples, as in the current pipeline.
- Persistence time τ_p is the lag where C = 1/e. Report it as a lower bound if it isn't reached, and as unresolved if it falls below 2 cadence steps.
- Also report a single-exponential fit for comparison.
- Short-lag angular diffusivity D_θ: initial slope of the unwrapped-heading MSD over the first 2–10 cadence steps. This stays measurable at strong input, where long-lag cosines read zero.

**A2. Size effect and noise tolerance.**
- Persistence ratio Ρ(f) = τ_p(largest) / τ_p(smallest), with bootstrap CI. Equivalently, use D_θ(small)/D_θ(large) where τ_p is censored.
- Noise tolerance f½(C): the input strength where τ_p falls to half its weakest-input value, interpolated in log f, with CI.

**A3. Geometric null.**
- Site position = heading angle × C/2π, in frames with a single qualified region. A peak-maximum version serves as sensitivity.
- Physical diffusivity D_x = D_θ·(C/2π)².
- Plot physical site MSD against lag to show whether the site diffuses freely or is confined (a plateau).
- Excess persistence = Ρ / (C_large/C_small)², where the geometric expectation is 9 for chemical rings and 9 for Spring.

**A4. Regime classification, per condition.**
- Fraction of second-half time spent in each state, using the existing descriptor definitions:
  - **single front:** exactly one qualified region
  - **multiple fronts:** two or more qualified regions
  - **low contrast:** contrast below 10% of basal
- The regime label is the class occupying more than 50% of time; otherwise the condition is "mixed".
- **Forced-state flag:** the matched mean control spends more than 50% of its second half with R < 0.1 or low contrast. Also flag a condition if E1 shows its regime persists as σ falls toward 0.

**A5. Protrusion-proxy statistics.**
- Track regions frame to frame by overlap. Use profile cadence, or scalar cadence where E2/E4 saved it. For each condition, report:
  - number of simultaneous regions (distribution and mean)
  - region lifetime (survival curve with censoring)
  - region width (physical, and as a fraction of circumference)
  - births per unit time per unit perimeter
  - birth position relative to the dominant region: angular distance distribution, distinguishing branching near the front from de novo births opposite it
  - takeovers: a newborn region becomes dominant, defined as Fig. 2 dominance D > 0.5 against the previous dominant region for at least 2 frames
  - dominant-heading turning-angle distribution at several frame intervals
- Sensitivities:
  - region threshold: min + 0.3, 0.5 or 0.7 × contrast
  - temporal downsampling ×1, ×2, ×4 and ×8, to mimic imaging frame intervals
  - spatial smoothing equal to one kernel length, to mimic optical resolution

**A6. Observable-to-prediction test.**
- Across all non-forced conditions, correlate each A5 statistic in small and large cells (and their large-to-small change) with log Ρ.
- Report Spearman correlations within and across models, and leave-one-model-out sign prediction.

**A7. Robustness.**
- First versus second half of the analysis window; late-quarter window.
- E4 horizon extension.
- Cadence downsampling.
- Results with and without forced-state conditions.

All cross-model comparisons use within-model ratios across size, never absolute native times.

## 4. Decision criteria

**Evaluable condition:** usable-pair coverage ≥ 0.5 in both sizes, and τ_p resolved or bounded. A model–regime result is decided on non-forced conditions. Report the forced-state-inclusive result alongside it.

| Hypothesis | Supported for a model if | Supported overall if |
|---|---|---|
| **H1: size buffers noise** | Ρ > 1 with CI excluding 1 at every evaluable f; no f with Ρ < 1 (CI); f½ rises with size (C20 and C60 CIs don't overlap) | Holds in ≥ 3 of 4 models |
| **H2: advantage window, then reversal** | Either: some evaluable f has Ρ < 1 with CI excluding 1. Or: Ρ falls ≥ 2-fold from its peak to a CI including 1, while the large-cell multiple-front fraction exceeds 0.5 over that range. | Holds in ≥ 2 models, and in A6 the multiple-front fraction (or region count) predicts log Ρ with \|Spearman\| ≥ 0.6, correct sign in ≥ 3 of 4 leave-one-model-out folds |
| **H3: model-specific failure** | At strong input (large-cell τ_p below half its weak-input value), the model's regime is multiple-front or low-contrast dominated | ≥ 2 distinct failure modes across models; the split survives forced-state exclusion and appears in E1 at fixed mean; fingerprints separate (region count and contrast distributions with non-overlapping CIs) |
| **Geometric qualifier** | **More than geometry:** Ρ above the geometric expectation (CI), or D_x falls with size (CI). **Geometric:** D_x flat within CI. **Less than geometry:** D_x rises with size. | Reported per model; not a gate |

H1 and H2 concern overlapping input ranges and can conflict. H3 concerns strong input only. Outcomes are therefore read as weak-input result × strong-input result.

## 5. Main figure construction

**Shared rules**
- Five panels at most, one message per panel. Legend text states the observed result.
- Persistence is shown as τ_p or Ρ, never a single-lag cosine.
- Every comparison shows small against large cells.
- Protrusion statistics are shown in their imaging-mimicking form (downsampled and smoothed).
- Forced-state conditions are greyed.
- Panel A is always example kymographs, small against large, taken from the regime the message is about.

**H1 (with H3 not supported)**
- *Message:* enlargement raises the noise tolerance of polarity direction; where shown, the gain exceeds geometry.
- *Panels:*
  - A. Kymographs at the transition strength.
  - B. τ_p against size at 2–3 strengths, with the C² line.
  - C. f½ against size for all models.
  - D. Physical site mobility against size.
  - E. Turning-angle and region-count summaries as the measurable signature of robustness.

**H2**
- *Message:* enlargement increases persistence only below a fragmentation threshold, and protrusion multiplicity shows which side of it a cell is on.
- *Panels:*
  - A. Kymographs from the advantage window and the reversal regime.
  - B. Regime map (f × size) with Ρ contours.
  - C. Ρ(f) per model, with the large-cell multiple-front fraction overlaid.
  - D. Protrusion fingerprints by regime.
  - E. Predictor scatter: multiple-front fraction against log Ρ.

**H3, combined with H1 at weak input (current leading guess)**
- *Message:* enlarged cells are more persistent; how they lose persistence under strong input is mechanism-specific and measurable.
- *Panels:*
  - A. Kymographs: weak-input small against large, plus the two large-cell failure modes.
  - B. τ_p against size at weak input with the C² line, all models.
  - C. f½ against size.
  - D. Large-cell regime fractions against f per model, showing fragmentation against contrast loss.
  - E. Fingerprints separating the failure modes, linked to the Fig. 2 mechanisms. Site mobility goes in an inset or to the supplement.

**H2 combined with H3**
- Use the H2 layout, and colour panel D by failure mode.

**No clean mapping (mixed, model-dependent or inconclusive)**
- Build a best-attempt figure around the most robust observation: a result that holds in ≥ 3 models and survives forced-state exclusion and the window checks.
  - A. Kymographs.
  - B. Full regime overview (Ρ heatmaps), so heterogeneity is visible rather than hidden.
  - C–D. The robust result or results.
  - E. Optional: the most promising observable from A6, even if below threshold, labelled as such.
- Supplement completeness becomes the priority. Every S-page is required, not optional.
- The S0 decision table carries a short decision memo:
  - the observed pattern in plain words, per model and regime
  - which criteria failed and by how much
  - 2–3 alternative main-figure framings, each with its supporting panels, weaknesses and what further runs would resolve it
