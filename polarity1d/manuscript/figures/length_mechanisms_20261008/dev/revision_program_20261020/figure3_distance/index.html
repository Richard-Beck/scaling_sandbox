Separation affects native competition differently across models

Rotating the same cue at a fixed circumference separates distance from enlargement. Under continuous forcing, Goryachev handover slows with separation, whereas Holmes 3 is nearly flat over its valid two-region placements. Wave-pinning and Spring show a near-edge increase followed by a broad plateau. Cue-free competition answers a different question: Wave-pinning, Holmes 3 and Spring can coarsen very slowly, with all completed-horizon lower bounds retained. These observations support model-dependent responses, rather than a universal absence of a distance effect in Wave-pinning.

The geometric translation requires measured native activity directions. Complete-region handover typically occurs around half the requested cue angle. A center-distance remap therefore does not itself measure time to turn through that angle, and enlargement does not guarantee greater directional persistence from these assays alone.

Measurements and native protocols

The amount is native active concentration integrated over a complete spatial region, after subtracting the common instantaneous minimum. Holmes 3 uses active Rac; the other models use their native active species. In a two-peak field, the periodic basins meet at the two intervening saddles. Boundary position comes from the local three-point parabolic minimum; the actual field is integrated as a piecewise-linear profile, including fractional cells. The boundary is not asserted to be the exact minimum of that piecewise-linear field. The two basins partition the complete native excess. Active flat or nonunique saddles are unscorable. A single moving region, a merged region, and cue-phase takeover receive explicit alternate outcomes.

Numerical sensitivity and limits

Two models, three gaps, one size each have fresh mesh and time-step controls. Both fine-grid basals independently qualify; all 12 A1 controls and 36 B controls pass native audits. A1 handover and B withdrawal-time errors are at most 0.0449%. All 36 B controls retain the seed status, primary-gap eligibility and winner;35 of 36 saved resolution brackets overlap. Goryachev C37.8, initial gap 4, ratio 1 shifts from 1040–1060 to 1080–1100 units on the finer mesh, about 4% in resolution time. This sensitivity is retained explicitly. The 24 B mirrored placements retain their outcomes.

Finite full-field stationarity and scalar/spatial fate remain separate. A finite cap supplies a lower bound, not proof that a state is permanently arrested. A2 calibrates only the five declared multipliers, so brackets are tested-duration brackets rather than an exact universal commitment threshold. No noise, dose sweep, moving cue, deBelly ring, or cell migration law enters this figure. Native model differences identify contrasting response classes; the assay does not by itself establish a unique causal molecular pathway.
