Two continued sheets for author review, separate from the rejected prototype. The organizing claim is that similar size-dependent reversal behavior can arise through distinct mechanisms predicting different challenge responses. The reversal trends remain in Figure 1; they are not replotted here.

Each model uses the same requested physical support 3, peak and 1000-unit duration across those sizes: WP 0.1, Goryachev 6.4, followed by 13000 cue-free native units. Both inputs add stimulus×inactive to active, with equal loss from inactive. The labels linking organization to response name conditional model predictions, not unique molecular identification or a causal allocation of reversal time. The native channels, doses, spatial discretization and clocks are not optical-actuator or reporter calibrations. The chemical grid has 201 cells at both sizes; the sampled input peak is 0.999388/0.997422 of the requested peak at L19/L39 (about 0.2% mismatch), while its physical integral is nearly unchanged. This small cue-sampling mismatch does not bound basal or other discretization errors; Goryachev L39's initial core has four sample centres and spatial convergence is not established by the retained checks. Thirteen thousand units of off-follow-up do not prove an asymptotic attractor. No velocity law or cell displacement is inferred. Small-loss recovery, analytic gain and evolved interventions remain supporting evidence, not the experimental headline.
