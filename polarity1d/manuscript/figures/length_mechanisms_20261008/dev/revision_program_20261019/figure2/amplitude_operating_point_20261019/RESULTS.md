# The earlier strength contrast depends on the tested operating range

The completed extension contains 20 new native full-field protocols and 12 exact prior histories. All 32 separately evaluated native field-tail tests pass. These are WP L9/L19 and Goryachev L9/L25 at fixed physical width 3, with WP pulse 240 or Gory pulse 1600 followed by 10000 cue-free units. Native basals, kinetics and stimulus channels are unchanged. Independent terminal/export review is pending.

Goryachev is substantially strength-sensitive below the previously tested 0.5–2 range. At L9, the sampled time to 25% original-region activity loss is (143.6, 143.7] at 0.03× peak, (53.5, 53.6] at 0.1× and (35.9, 36.0] at 0.3×. It then flattens to (29.6, 29.7] at 1× and (27.4, 27.5] at 3×. At L25 there is no sampled 25% loss by the finite 1600-unit pulse at 0.01×, 0.03× or 0.1×; the event occurs at (652.1, 652.2] at 0.3×, (393.4, 393.5] at 1× and (347.3, 347.4] at 3×. The 0.3× large-cell cue nevertheless fails retained scalar-strength replacement, so finite weakening is distinct from retention.

The realized native recruitment channel demonstrably compresses in the same operating range. Across the two Gory lengths, increasing requested peak tenfold from 0.03× to 0.3× increases the first-10-unit integral of stimulus × inactive by 4.03–4.04×; the next tenfold increase, 0.3× to 3×, increases it only 1.40–1.41×. Corresponding WP ratios are 9.90× and 8.86×. These comparisons use only newly retained dense histories; all 80 fine-versus-decimated saved-output quadrature checks pass the declared 0.1% tolerance. Exact cue-ROI inactive endpoints also show stronger depletion with larger Gory inputs. The integral is a native reaction-channel contribution, not net activity gain or matched cross-model optical exposure. Legacy 12 coarse channel integrals remain unqualified for this precision check.

Consequently, the earlier two-model difference in weakening-time sensitivity cannot by itself identify a mechanism. Substrate availability demonstrably compresses delivery through the native channel, but the fraction of the latency difference caused by it is not isolated from feedback, transport and incumbent organization. The expanded all-five experiment will characterize length series and native operating ranges rather than promote this two-model degree contrast into a unique signature.
