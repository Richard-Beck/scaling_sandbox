What the new range adds

Multiple regions also occur in WP, Holmes and Spring

The earlier two strengths did not span comparable changes in all models. At larger tested inputs, these three models spend substantial time with multiple activity regions and retain strong spatial contrast. Size changes how early and how frequently this happens.

A high region count can accompany a weak pattern

In large Goryachev cells, stronger input often produces small spatial variations relative to the original tall incumbent. Counting regions relative to each frame's own small contrast can therefore suggest many peaks even when the field has little basal-relative contrast.

Small Goryachev cells also show substantial contrast-qualified multiple-region activity. The data support size-dependent differences within the sampled input family, rather than a model-wide binary classification.

Every model keeps its original positive-input noise law and correlations. The factors are 0.0001, 0.0003, 0.001, 0.003, 0.01, 0.03, 0.1, 0.3 and 1. Scaling the whole signal increases its mean and fluctuation amplitude together. Model-native forcing and clocks are not calibrated across models.

In WP and Holmes, increasing multiple-region occupancy accompanies substantial long-lag memory loss in the largest cells. A short guide to the measurements

Multiple regions: more than one connected arc above the current minimum plus half the current contrast. The qualified fraction additionally requires contrast at least 10% of that case's initial basal contrast. Both fractions use every saved time in their denominator.

Activity vector: sum local activity with the ring's outward-normal directions. Its direction measures the global activity heading. Its magnitude R is relative to its own initial value and can exceed one; it is not cell speed.

Amount and coherence: A is total active amount relative to basal; Q is first-harmonic coherence relative to basal. R = AQ at each frame. Their separate changes explain why high R need not imply a single unchanged peak, and low R need not mean little total activity.

Memory: the mean cosine between usable global directions separated by a declared lag. The report retains eligible-pair coverage and full/first-half/last-half estimates, since organization can evolve during the finite experiment.
