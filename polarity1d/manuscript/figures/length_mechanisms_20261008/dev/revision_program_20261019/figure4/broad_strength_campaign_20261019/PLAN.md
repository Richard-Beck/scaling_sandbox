# Broad input-strength pilot for all four noisy-ring models

19 October 2026. The author agreed to scale the entire existing positive noise
signal. The question is whether the models show comparable sequences of local
organization as input strengthens, or retain qualitative differences. This is
a prospective campaign protocol, not a new main-figure layout or evidence of
discrete dynamical phases. Existing review figures remain unchanged.

## One common broad axis, with exact existing anchors

The common dimensionless input multiplier is

\[
f\in\{0.0001,\;0.0003,\;0.001,\;0.003,\;0.01,\;0.03,\;0.1,\;0.3,\;1\},
\qquad
I(q,t)=fA_m\exp\!\left[Z(q,t)-\frac12\right].
\tag{1}
\]

The Gaussian marginal variance and lognormal shape parameter stay at one.
Both the input mean and standard deviation increase in proportion to f.
The native amplitude references A_m are WP0.1, Goryachev6.4, Holmes1 and Spring4;
these are the original references, not retuned to obtain similar outcomes.
Equal f is not calibrated equal biochemical activation across models.

All three original F4 sizes and exact full basal states are retained: chemical
C20/40/60 at physical grid spacing0.05; Spring resting C0=1/2/3 with100/200/300
material cells and actual basal circumferences1.04140310035,2.04418072951 and
3.04516825240. No F3 preparation is substituted.

| Native duration |32000|3200|

| Canonical scalar / primary samples |6401 /801|6401 /801|

| Standard maximum solver step |5|0.125|

The exact original kernels, periodic zero mode, stationary OU knots and
variance-normalized interpolation are reused. This is the declared finite-knot
law, not exact continuous-time OU forcing between knots. Spring retains native
stretch/dilution/diffusion and the global mean-force circumference closure;
its instantaneous physical covariance width scales as C(t)/Cbasal. No sigma,
correlation-scale, moving-cue or velocity-law arm is included.

At every f, one deterministic spatially uniform input equals the **declared
ensemble mean fA_m**. It is not adjusted to a particular path's realized mean.
All existing no-added-input controls remain. Mean-induced loss of contrast or
first moment is recorded as its own result; noisy observations in such a
condition are not described as wandering of an otherwise intact mean-driven
pole. The matched means support interpretation without becoming a second
primary strength axis.

## First stage and possible replication

The two existing strengths,0.001 and0.01, retain all12 independent realizations
per model/size and all matched controls, totaling324 cases. A source/input/law/
solver/cadence proof links their original archives; no old trajectory is rerun
or overwritten. The seven new levels receive two independent seeds and one
mean control per model/size: **168 noisy +84 mean =252 new scientific workers**.
This yields576 available scientific cases, with sample sizes12 at the two
anchors and2 at new levels. Those two-seed points are exploratory, not a
well-estimated frequency of rare behavior.

Four additional numerical companions are described below. They are repeated
solves of existing pilot paths, never extra independent seeds. Thus the first
submission contains256 workers, not256 independent scientific cases.

After the pilot passes numerical/observation checks, replication allocation
will be frozen with the actual broad results. The complete uniform option is
12 seeds at all nine levels:1416 scientific cases,1092 new workers after exact
reuse, including the252 first-stage scientific workers. A smaller next stage
may prioritize adjacent sampled strength intervals where continuous occupancy,
coherence, amount or memory changes, but must retain every base level and
declare unequal precision. No model-specific “nice” strength is substituted
for the common grid. Sparse observations remain explicit.

The initial range is not required to make all models reach the same endpoint.
Persistence through f=1 is a bounded result. If the pilot suggests that the
common range misses basal-like behavior at its lower end, or behavior remains
strongly changing at its upper end, a root/review checkpoint may activate at
most one common extra decade: f=0.00001 and/or f=10 for **all four models and
all three sizes**. Reasons are recorded using the existing continuous readouts,
without erasing the base grid. This extension is not automatic and must not
force a desired collapse. No further range or midpoint ladder is authorized
by this first-stage protocol.

## Observable descriptions, not exclusive phase labels

All readouts retain per-seed observations, full/first-half/last-half windows
and actual sampling support. The inclusive windows are [0,T], [0,T/2] and
[T/2,T], containing6401/3201/3201 canonical samples; the shared midpoint does
not make the halves independent.

* **Local organization:** periodic current half-contrast region counts and
  native0.05-prominence peak counts, plus amplitude qualification. Report raw
  counts, counts when contrast is at least10% of initial basal contrast, and
  sensitivities at1% basal contrast and the old absolute contrast0.05. Also
  retain a prominence threshold5% of basal contrast. The latter is a fixed
  basal reference, not5% of a changing current profile. These thresholds are
  declared diagnostics, not proof of equivalent secondary-site strength.
* **Magnitude, amount and shape:** native/relative contrast, physical active
  amount M0/M0initial, raw q1 and q1/q1initial, q2 and R=|P|. Preserve the exact
  per-frame factorization R=(M0/M0initial)(q1/q1initial); population mean R is
  the mean of the product, not the product of separate means. Low R alone
  does not establish activity depletion, a uniform field or loss of local peaks.
* **Memory and site displacement:** full-vector and R-qualified direction
  correlations, endpoint coverage and the existing conditional site-coordinate
  diagnostics. Physical/reference-arc endpoints remain distinct from global
  direction and from travel or lineage. Chemical/Spring lag checkpoints are
  5/0.5,40/4,100/10,500/50,2000/200 and8000/800 in their respective native units.
* **Persistence of sampled organization:** occupancy and consecutive positive
  sample-run spans, with neighboring negative times and window censoring.
  A span is last minus first positive sample time, not proof of uninterrupted
  continuity or a mechanistic event duration. Do not report run counts as
  transition/event rates. Show actual native profiles for multiregion and
  low-R examples; allow descriptions to overlap.

Independent-seed uncertainty is attached to noisy condition summaries once
replication supports it. Deterministic means receive no bootstrap replicas.
Time origins and neighboring strengths are not independent biological replicates.
Increasing strength need not give a monotone sequence, and a finite logarithmic
grid only brackets sampled changes; it does not establish phase boundaries.

## Prospective upper-strength numerical and observation check

At f=1, the first pilot path at the largest size of **each** model saves10×
denser scalar and native-field observations throughout the same full horizon.
The four standard paths count toward the252 scientific workers. Each also
has one paired tight solve with the same exact seed, input generator, knots,
coupling grid and latent-field SHA. Only solver tolerances change to1e−10/
1e−12 and maximum solver step halves. A smaller solver step is not a finer
noise-knot grid. The finite forcing law is unchanged.

Canonical scalar/profile archives are exact every-tenth subsets of the dense
archives, kept separately. The whole observation record, not a differently
weighted selected time window, is compared. Standard/tight numerical checks
and coarse/dense observation checks are reported separately in each of the
three matched windows, never averaged across models to hide a failure.

Prospective tolerances are:

* Absolute occupancy difference≤0.02 for raw multiregion, amplitude-qualified
  multiregion, R<0.1 and contrast<0.1×initial contrast.
* For mean R, M0 ratio, q1 ratio and contrast ratio, absolute difference≤
  max(0.02,0.02×|reference mean|). The reference is standard for ODE checks
  and canonical coarse for observation checks.
* Direction-cosine difference≤0.05 at every declared lag checkpoint; eligible
  direction-pair fraction difference≤0.02. Both endpoints require R≥0.1.
  A direction estimate requires at least100 eligible pairs and fraction≥0.01
  in **both** compared records. Unsupported estimates remain unavailable;
  a change in availability is flagged rather than turned into a pass.
* For the90th percentile of sampled multiregion-run spans, the difference is
  at most max(two canonical scalar cadences,20% of the larger percentile).
  An empty run set is unavailable; appearance/disappearance is flagged.
  The same applies separately to the amplitude-qualified run descriptor.

Pointwise P differences and longest run spans are retained as diagnostics,
not sole convergence criteria. If run-span sensitivity fails while occupancy
and memory pass, retain that discrepancy and revise the duration descriptor/
cadence interpretation before replication. No event law is inferred. A passed
four-case screen qualifies this chosen pilot protocol, not every high-input
condition. Every worker independently retains finite/positivity, pool/amount
and physical-C checks. Numerical failures are not scientific outcomes or
right-censoring; they remain visible for explicit resolution.

## Runtime, storage and execution

Original one-CPU noisy workers took118–1013seconds for chemicals and250–1875
seconds for Spring. Prior Spring MaxRSS was below271MiB. Extrapolating the
same-size f=0.01 elapsed times gives a **28.7 worker-hour** baseline for252
scientific workers; this is an old-rate reference, not a timing prediction
at much higher input. A full uniform12-seed base grid has an analogous
169.7 worker-hour reference for all1092 new scientific workers.

All native work uses existing scaling_sandbox Python and SLURM QoSsmall:
ordinary workers1CPU/3GiB/2hours, and the eight dense standard/tight workers
1CPU/6GiB/4hours. There is no array concurrency throttle. The observed scheduler
QoS allows475 CPUs per user and supports these arrays; scheduler limits handle
allocation. Timeouts and failures are retained. Actual pilot runtime/memory
will determine later resource requests without changing scientific inputs.

Old per-case allocated storage gives a2.44GiB baseline for252 scientific
workers and10.57GiB for1092 new scientific workers; dense diagnostics and
stronger-input compression can change these values. Reserve approximately
5GiB for this first stage, measure allocated storage while it grows, and add
the new grouped run to root DATA_MANIFEST before handoff. Do not duplicate old
raw trajectories. Current art is preserved, and no new main figure is generated.
