Figure 4. Input regime shapes size effects on activity-heading memory and local organization. Columns show Wave-pinning, Goryachev, Holmes 3 and Spring ring. Each displayed model–size–input condition has twelve independent noisy realizations. Increasing f scales the whole positive activation signal, including its mean and fluctuation amplitude. Native clocks and forcing references differ across models. The same normalized physical-activity vector P as Figure 3 is used; R=|P|. Activity heading is distinct from cell migration.

A1–A4: native activity during a fixed short second-half window at the largest original size and f=0.1. Histories are declared morphology-descriptor medoids, with zero-based model-native concentration scales. White dashed vertical lines mark fixed snapshots at chemical time 18000 or Spring time 1800; matching actual profiles, basals and deterministic ensemble-mean controls are in the supplementary native-profile sheet. The displayed chemical window is 16000–20000 and Spring window 1600–2000. Spring position is material coordinate q; its evolving physical circumference remains in all activity moments.

B1–B4: pooled cosine of activity-heading differences at native lag 8000 for chemicals or 800 for Spring, conditional on both endpoint magnitudes R≥0.1. Narrow strips show eligible pairs divided by every available lag pair, so loss of usable direction remains visible. Whiskers are pointwise 95% percentile intervals from 2000 whole-seed resamples, paired across readouts and windows. Time samples are not treated as independent replicates. Full temporal curves with coverage and explicit sparse-support rules remain in the supplement and methods.

C1–C4: solid curves show the fraction of all scalar times with multiple periodic connected arcs above current minimum plus half current contrast (maximum minus minimum), additionally requiring contrast≥10% of the same case’s basal contrast. Dashed curves show the fraction below that contrast floor. These descriptors distinguish contrast-rich multiple-region organization from low-contrast states without assigning functional competition or exclusive phases. Summaries discard the first half, using chemical times 16000–32000 or Spring 1600–3200; window checks retain observed sensitivity rather than assuming equilibrium.

The broad-grid experiment comprises 816 noisy realizations, 108 matched-mean controls and 12 no-input controls: 936 scientific cases. Five strengths have twelve seeds; four outer strengths retain two exploratory seeds. Comprehensive support retains every model, size and strength, native fields, activity amount/coherence, controls, spatial widths and temporal/window evidence.
