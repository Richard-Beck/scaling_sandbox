# Measurement continuity across Figures 2–4

The figure sequence follows localized incumbent organization, its directional
sum on a ring, and the persistence of that heading under ongoing input.
These measurements address different questions and retain their own units.

Figure 2 integrates positive excess activity within a fixed initial incumbent
region. The current minimum, native-sample fifth percentile and frozen basal
minimum are explicit alternative background conventions. The fractional
half-contrast core used by the organization panels and the original whole-cell
worker core are reported separately. Localized excess is neither total active
amount nor a directional first moment. Spring uses evolving physical weights;
corrected linear deBelly retains its native nominal actuator and region
coordinates. Loss of localized excess describes organization or redistribution,
without identifying a lost molecular pool.

Figures 3 and 4 share the physical activity moments and initial normalization:

$$
M_n(t)=C(t)\left\langle u(q,t)e^{2\pi i n q}\right\rangle,
\qquad P(t)=\frac{M_1(t)}{M_1(0)},\qquad R(t)=|P(t)|.
\tag{1}
$$

Here, q is fractional physical arclength and C is actual circumference. The
complex normalization rotates the reference axes once and sets P(0)=1.
The phase gate R≥0.1 is shared. Spring resting circumference C₀ remains distinct
from instantaneous C and initial physical circumference Cᵦ.

Figure 4 separates amount and directional coherence:

$$
A(t)=\frac{M_0(t)}{M_0(0)},\qquad
Q(t)=\frac{|M_1(t)|/M_0(t)}{|M_1(0)|/M_0(0)},
\qquad R(t)=A(t)Q(t).
\tag{2}
$$

This identity holds frame by frame. Products of separate temporal means need
not equal the mean resultant magnitude. Normalized Q is distinct from an
unnormalized coherence fraction.

Figure 3 measures instantaneous native-RHS angular rate and saved-interval
secants during stationary cues. Figure 4 measures conditional lagged heading
cosine under noise. Its usable-pair fraction requires both lagged endpoints to
pass the phase gate; it differs from adjacent-interval rate coverage. A fixed
lagged cosine alone does not identify a persistence time. Neither quantity is
a cell-velocity law.

The biological motivation is WGD-associated enlargement and observed migration
persistence. Observed migration summaries, conditional OU estimates and these
1D activity-polarity measurements remain distinct. Their connection motivates
experiments rather than supplying an unmeasured migration result.
