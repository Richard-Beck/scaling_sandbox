# Figure R1. Cytosolic transport suppresses Wave-pinning domain drift beyond the geometric size effect

**A.** Drift-null activity kymographs at circumference $C=20$ and $60$, with panel width proportional to circumference, time upward and a shared greyscale (white zero, black one). The fixed physical diffusivity is $D_s=0.002$. A larger ring converts the same physical wandering into smaller heading changes. All quantities in this figure use native units.

**B.** Closed-form heading autocorrelation $C(\ell)=\exp[-(2\pi/C)^2D_s\ell]$ for A; light grey is C20 and dark grey C60. The heading-decorrelation rate is $k=-\log C(\ell)/\ell$. The geometric drift null therefore gives a ninefold smaller rate at C60.

**C.** Wave-pinning activity at input fraction $f=0.005$ (mean forcing divided by native reference input 0.1), with inactive diffusivity $D_v=10$ (native) or 1000. Each size uses native replicate 0 and the identical frozen Gaussian noise realization across diffusivities; time and activity scales are shared and width is proportional to circumference. Both sizes retain one moving activity domain; the paired fast-cytosol paths are shown without seed selection.

**D.** Whole-activity heading autocorrelation for C, light blue C20 and dark blue C60, with whole-seed bootstrap 95% bands. Heading is $\arg P$, with $P=M_1/M_0$; eligible endpoints have $|P|\geq0.1$. Dashed grey curves are the size-independent physical-drift guide $C_{20}(\ell)^{(20/60)^2}$. The largest absolute C60 departure from the fast-cytosol fixed-drift guide is 0.00537 over the observed lag grid.

**E.** Permanent shared domain displacement against edge-pulse dose (peak amplitude times duration 50), with light blue C20 and dark blue C60. Points are retained deterministic pulses; lines are through-origin fitted weak-dose slopes (input fractions at most 0.001), defining edge gain $G$. The C60 slope is smaller, and higher doses depart below the linear extrapolation.

**F.** Ring schematic with one active domain, an edge pulse and the two cytosolic paths between its edges, across the domain and around the back, shown as resistor segments in series around the closed loop. The schematic identifies the two transport distances that enter the conduction law.

**G.** Edge gain against circumference for seven inactive diffusivities. Here colour encodes $D_v$, light for slow and dark for fast; points are exact-ring adjoint calculations and lines are $G=(\lambda/2)/(1+C/C^*)$, where $C^*=2D_v/[\kappa\Delta u\,\theta(1-\theta)]$. Isolated-front values of $\kappa$, $\lambda$ and $\Delta u$ are fixed and $\theta$ comes from ring states; nothing is fitted. Native gain falls with size while the $D_v=1000$ C60/C20 gain ratio is 0.9978.

**H.** Local size exponent $\alpha=-d\log k/d\log C$ from the unchanged three-size round-3 estimator, at the three weak inputs available at all five sizes. Estimates are centred at C30, C40 and C50; each uses its three adjacent sizes, so all five sizes contribute. Circles denote native cytosol and open squares fast cytosol, with 95% whole-seed bootstrap intervals; the solid grey line is $\alpha=2+2(C/C^*)/(1+C/C^*)$ at native diffusivity and the dotted guide is 2. Inputs are retained in separate shared-scale facets. Native point estimates span 2.44–3. Fast-cytosol estimates span 1.78–2.22.
