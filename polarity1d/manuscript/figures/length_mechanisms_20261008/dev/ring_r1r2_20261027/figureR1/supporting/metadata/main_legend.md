# Figure R1. Cytosolic transport suppresses Wave-pinning domain drift beyond the geometric size effect

**A.** Drift-null activity kymographs at circumference $C=20$ and $60$, with panel width proportional to circumference, time upward and a shared linear viridis activity scale with its colourbar. The fixed physical diffusivity is $D_s=0.002$. A larger ring converts the same physical wandering into smaller heading changes. For each size pair, one common angular rotation places the initial dominant domain near mid-panel; the identical angular shift is used for both sizes at all times. Thin dotted seam lines mark any remaining wrap. All quantities in this figure use native units.

**B.** Closed-form heading autocorrelation $C(\ell)=\exp[-(2\pi/C)^2D_s\ell]$ for A; orange (#E69F00) is C20 and blue (#0072B2) C60. The heading-decorrelation rate is $k=-\log C(\ell)/\ell$. The geometric drift null therefore gives a ninefold smaller rate at C60.

**C.** Wave-pinning activity at input fraction $f=0.01$ (mean forcing divided by native reference input 0.1), with inactive diffusivity $D_v=10$ (native) or 1000. Each size uses native replicate 0 and the identical frozen Gaussian noise realization across diffusivities; the full post-burn window 16000–32000 is shown, time and linear viridis activity scales are shared across both diffusivities and sizes, and width is proportional to circumference. Both sizes retain one moving activity domain; the paired fast-cytosol paths are shown without seed selection.

**D.** Whole-activity heading autocorrelation for C over the full lag horizon 0–16000, orange C20 and blue C60, with whole-seed bootstrap 95% bands. Heading is $\arg P$, with $P=M_1/M_0$; eligible endpoints have $|P|\geq0.1$. Dashed grey curves are the size-independent physical-drift guide $C_{20}(\ell)^{(20/60)^2}$. The largest absolute C60 departure from the fast-cytosol fixed-drift guide is 0.0153 over the observed lag grid.

**E.** Permanent shared domain displacement against edge-pulse dose (peak amplitude times duration 50), with orange C20 and blue C60. Log–log axes retain the full dose grid. Points are retained deterministic pulses; each slope-1 line is its through-origin fitted weak-dose gain (input fractions at most 0.001); light shading marks that fit range, defining edge gain $G$. The C60 slope is smaller, and higher doses depart below the linear extrapolation.

**F.** Ring schematic with one active domain, an edge pulse and the two cytosolic paths between its edges, across the domain and around the back, shown as resistor segments in series around the closed loop. Arrows on both arcs show material moving from the far edge to the pushed edge. The schematic identifies the two transport distances that enter the conduction law.

**G.** Edge gain against circumference for seven inactive diffusivities. Every series is dark grey and directly labelled at its right end by $D_v$; the small key defines points as exact-ring adjoint calculations and lines as $G=(\lambda/2)/(1+C/C^*)$, where $C^*=2D_v/[\kappa\Delta u\,\theta(1-\theta)]$. Isolated-front values of $\kappa$, $\lambda$ and $\Delta u$ are fixed and $\theta$ comes from ring states; nothing is fitted. Native gain falls with size while the $D_v=1000$ C60/C20 gain ratio is 0.9978.

**H.** Local size exponent $\alpha=-d\log k/d\log C$ from the unchanged three-size round-3 estimator, at the three weak inputs available at all five sizes. Estimates are centred at C30, C40 and C50; each uses its three adjacent sizes, so all five sizes contribute. Black filled circles denote native cytosol ($D_v=10$) and black open squares fast cytosol ($D_v=1000$), with 95% whole-seed bootstrap intervals; the solid grey line is $\alpha=2+2(C/C^*)/(1+C/C^*)$ at native diffusivity and the dotted guide is 2. Inputs are retained in separate shared-scale facets. Native point estimates span 2.44–3. Fast-cytosol estimates span 1.78–2.22.
