# Jump supply, event response and size-dependent heading memory

**Encoding.** C20, C40 and C60 use orange #E69F00, green #009E73 and blue #0072B2 respectively; Spring sizes 1,2,3 use the same hues. Model identity is given by facets. Each kymograph uses linear viridis activity with its colourbar; kymographs of the same model share their scale. Every C20/C60 pair receives one common angular rotation to place its initial dominant domain near mid-panel; the same angle is used for both sizes throughout the window.

**A.** Jump-null kymographs at C20 and C60 with widths proportional to circumference and time upward. β appears above each facet. Uniform relocations arrive at $\lambda(C)=\lambda_0(C/40)^\beta$, $\lambda_0=1/7000$. β=1 represents perimeter supply and constant success; β=0 perimeter supply with success proportional to 1/C (or per-cell supply with constant success); β=−1 perimeter supply with success proportional to 1/C². All quantities use native units.

**B.** Analytic heading correlation $C(\ell)=\exp[-\lambda(C)\ell]$ for A, in the fixed size colours. The β=0 curves coincide; enlargement speeds loss at β=1 and slows it at β=−1.

**C.** Existing per-perimeter campaign activity reconstructed from stored active Fourier modes at fixed replicate 0. Both Goryachev and Wave-pinning use low rate and low amplitude. Both sizes share one model-specific linear activity scale and sampled time axis. Fourier ringing is clipped at zero for display only; signed reconstruction CSVs and the original Fourier data are unchanged.

**D.** Heading correlation for the exact conditions in C, pooled over four seeds, with endpoint $|P|\geq0.1$, $P=M_1/M_0$, and pointwise 95% whole-seed bootstrap bands. Orange is C20 and blue C60. Dashed grey is the fixed-physical-drift guide $C_{20}(\ell)^{1/9}$, undefined at nonpositive C20 correlation. Both facets retain the common lag axis 0–12000; full curves extend to the retained-record horizon in the source CSV.

**E.** Local exponent $\alpha$ against event amplitude/reference for per-perimeter supply, faceted by model. It is minus the three-size OLS slope of log mode-1 decorrelation rate against log actual mean circumference. Rates use the first half-height lag. All series are black: low rate solid/circle, medium dashed/square, high dotted/triangle. Filled markers and intervals denote resolved rates with 95% whole-seed uncertainty; open triangles with arrows denote bounds, including opposing arrows for two-sided or unbounded slopes. Pending estimates are × on the thin band below each axis, never at an assigned α. Grey guides are α=2,0,−1. Goryachev α ≥ 3.645 (lower_bound); Wave-pinning α = 1.64 (resolved); Holmes α = 0.8958 (resolved); Spring α = -0.1375 (resolved).

**F.** The same exponent compares supply arms, in black: per-perimeter solid with filled circles, per-cell dashed with open circles. Facets identify model and event rate; bounds, pending band, intervals and guides match E. Per-cell outer sizes use the fixed C40 arrival intensity. The shared Spring C40 centre (size 2) keeps its original state-dependent hazard; it is not rerun. Wave-pinning per-cell minus per-perimeter α ranges from 0.7211 to 1.042 across 7 matched resolved conditions (earlier check about +1). Spring per-cell minus per-perimeter α ranges from 0.2705 to 1.108 across 9 matched resolved conditions (earlier check about +1).

**G.** Event-triggered excess $\mathcal E(w)$, the event-centred mean of $1-\cos\Delta\theta$ minus its random-window mean in the same runs, versus window width w=2–64 original frames on a log axis. Each Δθ compares the circular mean headings of the w frames immediately before and after a centre; a sample exactly at the centre is excluded. Conditions are low rate/high amplitude. Orange and blue denote C20/C60 (Spring 1/3). Chemical models use per-perimeter events regenerated and verified against checkpoints; Spring uses logged per-cell outer-size events. Each seed contributes 100 uniformly timed controls per scorable event at each w, over the fully observed run support, retaining incidental events. Lines join measured excesses; bands are pointwise 95% whole-seed bootstrap intervals, resampling event and control contributions jointly. Zero means equal event and background averages; signed excess and window-dependent size differences remain visible. No shared-centre value is required.
