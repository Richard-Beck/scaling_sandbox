## What the figures support

The order is scientifically coherent. Incumbent excess amount grows through different width/peak changes; native local input gain and conditional local repair then describe two specific functions of those prepared states. Finite competition tests their responses to a substantial opposing cue without assuming that either coefficient determines the outcome. In particular, weak size dependence of gain in Wave-pinning or Holmes does not prevent a strong change in their retained regional activity.

The common recovery measurement is a response to the declared smooth incumbent activity-loss direction, with a paired native sham and one fixed window per model. It is not generic stability, a common physical-time calibration, or a causal partition of replacement time. deBelly changes Rac alone because it has no explicit inactive Rac pool; this initially preserves its other chemical and mechanical organization. Its modest local Rac repair trend cannot establish how difficult that slower organization is to rewrite.
