Each model overlays two different lengths in every native-field facet. Blue always marks the shorter cell; orange marks the longer cell. Choose a patch condition, then use the slider or enter a common actual time measured from simultaneous pulse onset. Solid curves show the current fields; dashed curves show their starting states. Colored dotted vertical lines mark each current profile’s x50: the spatial coordinate with half its integrated field on either side.

All three inputs are localized raised-cosine patches at the right end: 20% of the instantaneous domain with fixed peak; fixed physical width with fixed peak; or fixed physical width with peak adjusted to match the 20% patch's spatial integral. These are finite pulses with independently selected durations at the two lengths, followed by 10,000 units of recovery. They are not gradient stimuli.

Native amplitudes are unchanged. Spatial position is divided by nominal length (Spring uses material fraction); this aligns spatial domains without normalizing concentrations. Each field's axes stay fixed across both lengths, all times and all three conditions.

x50 uses the integral of nonnegative native values over the plotted grid, not the peak or half-height. Profiles with genuine negative values, zero/negligible integral, or completed observations have no marker; the facet readout says why. Only roundoff negatives within 1e-12 + 1e-10 × maximum absolute value are treated as zero for this diagnostic. Global observables have no spatial x50.
