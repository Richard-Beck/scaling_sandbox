Preparation and display notes
  • The reversal cue is the native right-end raised-cosine patch, extra peak 0.1, switched on at t = 0 and held continuously through t = 300 s. The t = 0 field is the exact pre-cue checkpoint. Basal kinetics remain active.
  • Fixed support is 3 µm at both lengths. Relative support is 3 µm at L = 15 and 6 µm at L = 30. The two L = 15 experiments therefore coincide.
  • L = 15 uses the exact selected, corrected basal-release checkpoints. L = 30 is absent from the retained length grid and is prepared freshly from native rest, separately for each width, with the same finite-left-patch duration search, strongest-endpoint selection and verified basal release. No profile is interpolated, reflected or substituted. Frozen L = 31 field scales are used only for L = 30 convergence diagnostics.
  • Active and inactive use separate concentration axes. Each field shares one fixed axis across all cases and all times; inactive zoom optionally changes its axis at each snapshot, still shared across cases. The inactive axis is offset from zero to expose its smaller changes. Profiles are native concentrations without renormalization.
  • Shading marks cue support, not a concentration field. Dashed lines show each case’s own onset state. Solid profiles join the 201 recorded finite-volume cell centers; snapshots are native solver output at the displayed time without time interpolation.
  • These continuous-cue trajectories are a diagnostic experiment. They do not apply the finite-pulse withdrawal/recovery response gates of the manuscript comparison.
