## Scientific purpose

Larger cells hold heading longer. Build simple peak drift/jump nulls, measure
the same quantities in four unchanged models, and measure drift/jump ingredients
directly. k = −ln C(lag)/lag; k ∝ circumference^(−alpha). Fixed physical drift
gives alpha=2; uniform same-rate jumps alpha=0; jump rate ∝ perimeter alpha=−1.
Mixtures add (2pi/circumference)^2 Ds + lambda(1−mean cos jump angle).
