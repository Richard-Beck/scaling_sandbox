# Standalone Figure 2 v2

Panel A retains only the 160-s single-stimulus experiment and facets the four historical model arms by row, now simulated through 10,000 s. Markers show the first sustained steady-state point only for polarized trajectories. Panel B replaces the former kymographs with polarization time versus length and includes only polarized full-model Rac and MCA results. Panels C-D use intact-model beta variants at 0x, 1x, and 2x the Julia-default beta=0.2; alpha0=beta0=1 and alpha=0.2.

The polarity coordinate is the interpolated relative half-mass position x50/L. A steady state is called at the first time after the relevant stimulus at which the coordinate enters and remains within 0.002L of the median over the final 1,000 s. The event is censored unless at least 1,000 s of confirmation remain. Panel C points mark this event: circles denote polarized steady states and crosses denote unpolarized steady states, using a 0.02L displacement from center as the cutoff. Panel D includes only polarized, uncensored steady states. Panels A, C, and E use compact y-axis ranges shared by field within each panel.

Panel E shows beta=1x trajectories for 150-, 300-, and 600-s mirrored counter-cues applied only after Rac and bound MCA have settled. Panel F summarizes a brute-force 50-duration linearly spaced sweep from 10 to 1000 s for beta=0x, 1x, and 2x. A reversal requires the final field to settle within 0.002L and lie within 0.05L of the reflected pre-cue polarity on the opposite side of 0.5L. Both pre- and post-cue states must also lie at least 0.02L from the center so numerical sign changes of unpolarized states are not counted. Points without a successful duration are omitted and lines break across them.

This parallel rerun changes only the four activation parameters requested: alpha0=1, beta0=1, alpha=0.2, and beta=0/0.2/0.4 for the 0x/1x/2x arms. All other model parameters, cue amplitudes, protocols, numerical settings, and analysis criteria are inherited unchanged from the existing v2 workflow.
