# Agreed update to the OU fitting experiment

Agreed October 3, 2026. Use the enhanced migration literature data to update the existing OU experiment in three ways. Keep the existing experimental groups and model comparisons.

1. **Upgrade the Xuan MSD fits.** Fit both published population MSD curves across early, intermediate and late lags, replacing the single late-lag target. Retain sampled mean speed as a fitting target. Give the MSD curve a balanced contribution to the fit rather than counting every digitized pixel as an independent observation.

2. **Refine estimates elsewhere.** Replace rough figure readings with the reviewed digitized estimates for the measurements already used in Xuan, Mallin, Jemaà and Hosny. Preserve the distinction between means, medians, quartiles and uncertainty measures. Keep existing reserved measurements as prediction checks.

3. **Use the Xuan movie tracks for diagnostic checks.** Compare measured trackplots and motion statistics with simulated tracks sampled at the same frame resolution. Check displacement distributions, temporal correlations, prediction residuals, and slow or fast episodes. In particular, ask whether the observed fluctuations match the model's expected velocity noise and memory. Account for centroid measurement uncertainty, and keep physical-time comparisons conditional on the unresolved movie timing. These tracks are representative diagnostics, not replacement population fitting targets.

No new groups, pooled clone analyses, additional model mechanisms or expanded fitting campaigns are part of this update. This note records the agreed disposition; it does not change observation tables or run fits.
