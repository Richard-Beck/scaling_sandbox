# Mallin 2023 Figure 2 extraction and movie feasibility

This package measures the published 2023 version-of-record Figure 2 (DOI 10.1007/s10585-023-10216-8). Source PNG, article snapshot, extracted article text and publisher streaming MP4s remain immutable in the parent directories. `protocol.json` records their hashes and software. No author numerical data were available locally. No fitting or population retracking was performed.

## Figure measurements

The original 2003 × 999 PNG was visually inspected with its caption before extraction. The final full-size annotated overlay and both movie contact sheets were reviewed. All coordinates refer to native source pixels, origin upper left; crops are stored in `calibration.json` as [left, top, right-exclusive, bottom-exclusive]. Axes are linear. The six marginal panels are Figure 2A, 2B and 2D, each containing non-PACC and PACC groups. Figure 2E and 2F provide paired area–motility scatter points within each group. Figure 2C is not digitized into trajectories because crossing spider-plot paths do not identify cell/time correspondences.

`digitized_points.csv` retains 321 resolved glyph positions, calibrated values, panel-local point IDs and uncertainty fields. It contains 201 marginal dot positions and 120 paired area–metric positions. These are incomplete plotted distributions, not exact cell-level source data. Do not calculate an unbiased population mean, variance, correlation or sample size from this selected subset: crowded regions have preferentially missing points. `coverage.csv` gives recovered counts and unresolved-region counts by panel and condition. Caption n = 50 and n = 49 explicitly describes Figure 2C; assuming it also applies to every scatterplot is unverified. In particular, PACC Figure 2E reaches about 460 um, whereas the PACC 2B maximum is about 270 um. This mismatch prevents assuming identical cohorts or linking cells across those panels.

The extractor thresholds grayscale at <128. Scatterplots use a 5 × 5 elliptical morphological opening to remove thin regression lines. Connected components qualify only within strict single-dot dimensions and area ranges (recorded in the script); merged, line-connected or oversized regions are rejected. No merged region is split or assigned a multiplicity. Final visual review additionally excludes two unusually large marginal glyphs in 2A PACC at native coordinates (290.5, 296.5) and (290.4545, 370.3636) as possible merged dots; their dark areas are 12 and 11 pixels. They remain in the unresolved-region table, and regeneration preserves these coordinate-based exclusions. Point IDs are regenerated in ordinate order. `unresolved_regions.csv` preserves rejected-region bounds; these include summary-line components and some conservative rejections of distorted single dots, so region count is not missing-cell count. No multiplicity is inferred from marker area. Green circles show accepted points, orange boxes unresolved regions, cyan boxes crop bounds and blue crosses central bars; scatter point numbers match IDs in order of increasing image y. Marginal dots are identified by their pixel coordinates.

`measurements.csv` records six horizontal central bars. The source caption and methods do not identify their statistic, and no error bars accompany them. They are retained as “central bar, statistic unspecified,” never converted into means or medians. Bar coordinates are the midpoint of their solid horizontal raster rows. Values (non-PACC, PACC) are accumulated distance approximately 319.7 and 166.0 um; Euclidean distance 38.7 and 67.6 um; directness 0.132 and 0.413. These replace visual approximations only at the level of a digitized central bar. The earlier literature summary's 300–310 um accumulated-distance reading is lower than the reviewed 319.7 um bar reading; no downstream observation table was modified.

Reading sensitivity is +/-1 source pixel, corresponding to approximately 4.10 um in 2A, 0.98 um in 2B, 0.00327 in 2D, 0.55 um and 1.81 um in the respective 2E scatterplots, and 0.00227 and 0.00365 in the 2F scatterplots. Area reading sensitivity is approximately 20.7, 388.6, 20.3 and 365.9 um2 in the four area scatterplots. Axis endpoint sensitivity uses independent 1-pixel perturbations propagated through the linear transform and is stored separately. These quantify raster/calibration sensitivity, not biological variation, statistical confidence intervals, SEM or IQR. Decimal digits in CSVs preserve numerical transforms, not biological precision.

`validate.py` checks source hashes, pixel–value round trips and repeat central-bar row readings. `threshold_sensitivity.csv` repeats extraction at thresholds 80 and 180. Changes in point counts reflect boundary and merge sensitivity; matches within 1.5 px quantify stability of recoverable positions. This check does not certify completeness or turn ambiguous dots into observations.

## Movie feasibility

`movie_frame_diagnostics.csv` records every adjacent decoded-frame grayscale difference and playback position; `movie_candidate_states.csv` proposes image-state groups at mean absolute difference >0.2 grayscale units. The movie contact sheets show six native decoded frames resized only for viewing. No coordinates were measured from resized previews.

Both streams decode at 1024 × 768 and 25 fps. Movie 1 has 178 encoded frames (7.12 s playback), with 160 exact unique decoded images and 18 exact duplicates. Movie 2 has 198 frames (7.92 s), 197 exact unique images and one duplicate. Tiny compression differences make exact uniqueness unsuitable for counting acquisition frames. Large-image-change thresholds 0.2, 0.5 and 1.0 all give 43 candidate image states for non-PACCs and 48 for PACCs. These usually begin about every four streaming frames. Low thresholds 0.02–0.1 instead count encoding differences and are unreliable for acquisition indexing. Candidate state counts are not definitively original acquisition-frame counts. They do not establish coverage of every 30-minute interval in the reported 24-hour experiment (48 intervals, typically 49 images including both endpoints). The non-PACC stream especially cannot be assigned the advertised full-duration cadence without recovering original frame mapping.

The article explicitly describes 10× imaging every 30 min for 24 h and uniform 20% FBS for the associated Figure 2 motility experiment. Non-PACCs are untreated PC3-luc parental cells, PACCs are cisplatin-selected enlarged cells after the specified recovery period; this is not an isogenic WGD contrast. No readable scale bar, pixel-size metadata or experimental timestamps are visible in the sampled frames. A 10× objective alone cannot determine um/pixel for a resized publisher rendition. Thus physical displacement and speed cannot currently be measured defensibly from these streams.

Movie 1 contains dense multicell clusters, contacts and apparent division; cell or nuclear centroid identity becomes ambiguous during rearrangements. Sparse individual cells may support manually reviewed pixel tracks, with exclusions at cluster contacts/division. Movie 2 has several separated large cells, so its image quality appears adequate for manually reviewed pixel tracks of selected cells; large protrusions and strong shape changes make whole-cell centroids unstable. Nuclear centers, where visible, would require a consistent tracking definition and uncertainty. Some cells contact the chamber boundary or enter/exit the field. No track identities or usable intervals have been audited across every candidate state, so no coverage count is claimed. Static chamber borders in movie 2 could assist a future drift audit; population motion should not be subtracted as drift.

Decision: **conditionally feasible for representative pixel tracking after acquisition-state review; physical-time/calibrated tracking remains unresolved**. Prefer original WMVs plus camera/pixel calibration and acquisition mapping before quantitative comparison. The movies cannot be assumed to contain all 50/49 Figure 2 cells or independent experimental replicates. No new downloads, segmentation campaign, invented cadence, automatic interpolation or biological classification by size alone were introduced here.

## Reproduction and retention

Run from repository root using the existing `cpose` environment (checked before use):

```
conda run -n cpose python literature_review/migration_data/mallin_2023/extraction/scripts/extract.py
conda run -n cpose python literature_review/migration_data/mallin_2023/extraction/scripts/validate.py
```

The first command regenerates calibrated CSVs, calibration/protocol JSON, review overlays and movie diagnostics; the second writes validation and sensitivity outputs. OpenCV performs serial lightweight decoding; no compute-heavy segmentation or GPU work is required. Outputs are final review artifacts, with no temporary frame dumps or working directories. `__pycache__` is unnecessary and not retained.
