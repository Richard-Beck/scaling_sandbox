# Manuscript vision

Current author direction as of 17 October 2026.

## Scientific argument

Cell enlargement changes polarity organization and the dynamics of replacing an
established polarity. The theoretical results suggest a basis for enhanced
migration persistence in WGD-associated enlarged cells. Existing migration data
support this hypothesis. Mechanistic explanations should lead directly to
specific measurements that could reveal the corresponding behaviour in cells,
even where those measurements would not uniquely confirm the explanation.

The existing manuscript figures supply much of this foundation. The paper should
retain their comparative polarity results, mechanistic content and migration
evidence while developing the connection between them. Model- and
stimulus-dependent differences, including nonmonotone responses, belong in that
argument.

The work underway on 17 October develops the combined mechanism and measurement
component. Its evolving linear and ring analyses, assay definitions and panel
layouts should be understood within that role. A candidate layout does not
define the structure of the whole manuscript. Mechanistic observations and
measurement proposals should be presented together so that each proposed
measurement has an explicit theoretical motivation.

The migration component should distinguish observed displacement and directness
from conditional OU persistence estimates. Likewise, polarity-replacement cue
durations and migration persistence are different quantities; their proposed
relationship is the hypothesis connecting the theoretical and empirical work.
