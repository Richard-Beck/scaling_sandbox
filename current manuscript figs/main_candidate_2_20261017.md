# Candidate 2, version 2: activation does not determine the spatial outcome

This provisional six-panel candidate extends the [archived four-panel version 1](main_candidate_2_v1_20261017.md). It is a result draft for fresh-context critique, not a selected final main figure.

![Replacement, merger and retained sites](../polarity1d/manuscript/figures/length_mechanisms_20261008/dev/main_candidate_2_v2_20261017/main_candidate_2_v2.png)

## Proposed message

All three challenged regions activate, but later spatial outcomes differ: replacement at C = 30 µm, d = 12 µm; merger at C = 40 µm, d = 12 µm; and two sites retained through the observed cue-free window at C = 40 µm, d = 16 µm. These outcomes cannot be reduced to challenger activation or a single clock measuring loss in the original core. Raw spatial histories are needed to distinguish loss at the old position, bridging between sites and coexistence.

## Why these panels convey the message

The top row shows full raw active-Rac histories through 4000 s, preserving 1000 s with cue and the first 3000 s after withdrawal. The bottom row compares basal, withdrawal and 13,000-s cue-free endpoint profiles. It exposes the distinction between a final cap near the cue, a cap between the two sites and two narrower caps. All columns use one physical frame with the original incumbent at zero, native µM/µm/s units and one color scale. Gray space in the first column is outside its 30-µm periodic interval; no 40-µm geometry is implied there.

The first comparison changes circumference from 30 to 40 µm while keeping physical cue separation at 12 µm. The second keeps the same 40-µm circumference and **exact same N = 1600 full basal state** while moving the cue from 12 to 16 µm. The two periodic routes are respectively 12/18, 12/28 and 16/24 µm. All three runs use matched physical cell width h = 0.025 µm, unchanged native kinetics/diffusion, fresh one-site basal preparation and the same support-3-µm, peak-1-µM/s cue for 1000 s, followed by 13,000 s without cue.

The [native peak-width export](../polarity1d/manuscript/figures/length_mechanisms_20261008/dev/main_candidate_2_v2_20261017/supporting/data/native_peak_widths.csv) describes the residual original-side regions: local half-prominence widths change from 12.38 to 3.94 µm at C30,d12; from 16.54 to 8.16 µm at C40,d12; and from 16.54 to 8.23 µm at C40,d16 between basal and withdrawal. Basal peak concentrations are near 1.484 µM. These observations motivate a residual-incumbent capacity or architecture hypothesis. The similar C40 residual widths do not themselves explain their different fates. They do not identify a width threshold or separate material effects from geometry. Width is measured at half each contemporaneous local peak's prominence using its valleys in a periodically tiled native profile, not a frozen basal gate.

## Support, selection and scope

This is a combined size-and-separation comparison, not isolated incumbent-width causality. C30 and C40 differ in total Rac/Rho material (225/93 versus 300/124 µM·µm), basal incumbent width, the complementary route, local basal chemistry and cue fraction of the circumference. The native pool densities remain 7.5/3.1 µM. Active Rac alone is not conserved. The figure is a within-model chemical pattern result and does not demonstrate migration persistence or a biological WGD calibration.

All predefined size-path cases are disclosed in the [size report](../polarity1d/manuscript/figures/length_mechanisms_20261008/dev/holmes_size_bridge_20261017/supporting/metadata/RESULTS.md): C30,d12 gives replacement; C40,d16 and C50,d20 retain two sites over the recorded window. C50 is supporting rather than omitted as a contrary outcome. The separate fixed-C40 d12 result gives merger. The [original three-model distance pilot](../polarity1d/manuscript/figures/length_mechanisms_20261008/dev/chemical_ring_distance_20261017/supporting/metadata/RESULTS.md) retains every original distance and numerical control; no failed or unresolved case is relabelled as a biological outcome.

The **figure uses fine-grid h = 0.025 µm trajectories**. The [challenge mesh report](../polarity1d/manuscript/figures/length_mechanisms_20261008/dev/holmes_distance_robustness_20261017/supporting/metadata/MESH_RESULTS.md) finds the same d12/d16 endpoint modes at h = 0.05/0.025 µm, with closely matching final profiles but larger transient differences around d12 merger. Exact merger time is not claimed. Separately, the [conserved-Rac imbalance continuations](../polarity1d/manuscript/figures/length_mechanisms_20261008/dev/holmes_distance_robustness_20261017/supporting/metadata/IMBALANCE_RESULTS.md) use the **N = 800, h = 0.05 µm d16 endpoint**, not the fine-grid states plotted here: ±1% local active-Rac redistribution and a matched unperturbed control retain two sites for a further 13,000 s. This supports finite-window robustness under that bounded perturbation, not asymptotic or global branch stability. Slow competition, exact transition locations and causal attribution remain open.
