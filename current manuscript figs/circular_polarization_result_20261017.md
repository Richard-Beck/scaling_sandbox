# Result: one polarized site in each circular construction

![Five circular-model preparations: native starts, cue-withdrawal profiles and qualifying cue-free profiles.](../polarity1d/manuscript/figures/length_mechanisms_20261008/dev/circular_polarization_overview_20261017/circular_polarization_overview.png)

All five declared circular constructions produce a single cue-free polarized
site at the first tested parameter setting. This establishes a starting point
for the proposed challenger-location experiment. It is preparation evidence,
not a main-text mechanistic claim or a distance result.

Wave-pinning, Goryachev and Holmes retain their native local reactions,
diffusivities and pool densities on a periodic circumference-40 domain. Fresh
preparations qualify on both 400- and 800-cell grids after a 10-unit cue and a
10000-unit cue-free holdout. Cue support is 3, with peaks 0.1, 0.1 and 1.
These preparation doses differ from the current interval response protocol.

Spring uses a declared circular global-extension analogue. It retains the local
reaction/force laws, diffusion, extension-feedback coefficients and dilution;
mean force around the ring replaces endpoint sampling. At resting circumference
2, 200 cells and cue peak 4/support 0.2/duration 10, one site remains after the
full-field and circumference settling checks and 10000-unit holdout. Final
circumference is 2.04418. See the
[derivation and result](../polarity1d/manuscript/SPRING_RING_CLOSURE_20261017.md).

deBelly uses a cue-created zero-width cut, retained after cue removal, and
tangential mechanics on a fixed circular reference loop. Reflection symmetry
of the single site permits an exact half-loop computation with one shared
full-loop MCA budget. Circumference is 62, with 402 full-loop-equivalent cells;
cue peak is 3.6 for 60 s, over 20% of the full circumference. The original
20000-s capped qualification was unresolved because an early tension window
narrowly missed its criterion. A separate exact-state continuation qualifies
the same preparation through passing 18000–20000-s prewindows and a prospective
20000–30000-s holdout. No parameters or tolerances changed. See the
[protocol](../polarity1d/manuscript/figures/length_mechanisms_20261008/dev/distance_ring_protocol_20261017/debelly_single_incumbent_prototype.md)
and [continuation audit](../polarity1d/manuscript/data/runs/debelly_planar_loop_preparation_20261017/continuation/independent_audit.json).

The five raw-amplitude panels make the observed patterns inspectable without
equating model-native signal scales. Each site is centred for display only;
Spring uses each phase's actual circumference, and deBelly's two flanks form
one site. The geometry assumptions belong in the caption. Shared preparation
does not yet determine how challenger activation, incumbent weakening or
post-cue survival depends on separation.

No challenger trials have contributed to this result.

**Fresh review.** The [independent critique](reviews/circular_polarization_r1.md)
accepts this preparation milestone and recommends keeping the overview as
development evidence. Its main qualification concerns deBelly: the preparation
was evolved in the reflection-symmetric subspace, and the mirrored full-loop
export alone omits the four boundary-controller fields. The asymmetric
two-arc adapter must transfer those fields from the complete half-loop restart,
preserve the shared incumbent before opening the challenger cut, and retain
gap-only controls. These requirements are being applied to the distance pilot.
