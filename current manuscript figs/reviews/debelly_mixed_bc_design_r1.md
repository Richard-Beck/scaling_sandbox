# deBelly mixed two-arc boundary design: preflight review

17 October 2026. Bounded design review of [DESIGN.md](../../polarity1d/manuscript/figures/length_mechanisms_20261008/dev/debelly_shared_chemical_arcs_20261017/DESIGN.md), the [native finite-volume source](../../polarity1d/manuscript/code/core/models/debelly.py), and the [earlier disconnected-arc pilot](../../polarity1d/manuscript/figures/length_mechanisms_20261008/dev/distance_ring_protocol_20261017/debelly_two_arc_distance_result.md). No adapter inspection, new integration, parameter selection or retuning was performed.

**Verdict: defensible first-pass mixed closure, with no mathematical conservation blocker apparent in the written design.** It implements common *evolving diffusive face traces*, rather than a fixed concentration clamp or a projection that equates neighboring cell averages. Independent bound MCA and mechanics follow the user's proposed distinction. The six prescribed runs can proceed after the stated preflight passes; this review does not certify an unseen implementation or initial checkpoint.

The two half-cell resistances and weighted trace in Eq. (1) are correct. The same interface flux must enter one arc and leave the other in a joint implicit solve. For equal grid spacing and diffusivity this is exactly the ordinary interior diffusion conductance. A common face value can coexist with unequal adjacent cell averages and finite diffusive gradients. Its algebraic dependence on evolving cell values satisfies the proposed evolving-boundary interpretation without introducing cap storage. Rac and Rho reaction sources may differ on the two flanks because tension and bound MCA differ; that does not invalidate a shared diffusive trace.

**The unbound-MCA qualification is substantive and already substantially explicit.** Its interface diffusion is reciprocal, while its native endpoint advection independently removes or supplies material using each implicit boundary-cell donor concentration. Those donor values need not equal the reconstructed diffusive trace. This is a split numerical closure with branch-to-omitted-region advection and global replacement; it is not continuity of the complete advective–diffusive flux through a resolved cap. It is honest to call this “shared diffusive chemical traces with native unresolved protrusion advection/replacement,” as the design does. Calling it unrestricted continuity of every chemical boundary process would overstate what was implemented. A common advective cap concentration would be a different model choice, not a prerequisite for this disclosed first pass.

The MCA ledger in Eq. (2) correctly retains the native sequential-exchange residual: bound MCA binds from old unbound MCA, whereas unbound MCA loses binding from its updated value. Reciprocal unbound diffusion cancels only in the sum over both arcs. Global correction restores the physical bound-plus-unbound pool, while still allowing redistribution between arcs. Endpoint transport and correction must remain **signed**: membrane relaxation can produce inward boundary velocity, so “loss” and “replacement” need not be positive. Total conservation cannot establish local transport realism or a molecular travel time.

**Essential preflight gates and clarifications:**

- Use the native diffusion coefficients in the new links. In this source Rac and Rho diffusion in the actual time-evolution operator is the deactivation rate times the named diffusion parameter: 0.04 × 0.5 = 0.02 µm²/s for each, not 0.5 µm²/s. Preserve the native reaction staggering as well as interior diffusion. A coefficient or staging mismatch is a launch blocker.
- Verify the complete mirrored incumbent state, including raw and relaxed endpoint controllers and orientation signs. If both boundary-cell chemicals and all mirrored mechanical/controller states agree, symmetry makes the added incumbent diffusion link carry zero flux throughout the native step, and the Rac sensor is unchanged. Equality of the initial chemical cells alone is insufficient proof. A material discrepancy in the planned full-state native-versus-mixed comparison is a launch blocker; the existing preparation should not silently be requalified or replaced.
- Restrict “native identity for equal arcs” to identical/mirrored arc states, controllers and forcing. Equal lengths alone cannot imply identity when the new chemical links carry nonzero flux. Test uniform-field preservation for the diffusion operator, or for a compatible homogeneous rest state; arbitrary compressible native advection need not preserve a uniform concentration.
- Close the pre-replacement per-arc ledger with implicit interface and donor values, then account separately for the length-weighted uniform correction. Test reciprocal cancellation, positivity and coordinate reversal. Interpret “no material impulse” as no mass insertion or cell-average projection. Opening the mechanical gap still changes endpoint dynamics and can retain the earlier pilot's membrane-velocity jump; matched cut-only controls remain necessary.

Different local commands are an acceptable disclosed consequence of common Rac but separate tension. Chemical coupling therefore creates a shared chemical junction, not a single mechanical protrusion controller. The old pilot's arc-wide Rac segregation motivates examining spatial fields, but does not establish what the new construction will do. Outcomes should retain the proposed full-field and budget checks and remain a bounded construction test, without retuning or a claim about enlargement-associated migration persistence.
