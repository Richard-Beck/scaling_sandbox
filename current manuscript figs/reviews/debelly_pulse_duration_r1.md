**Verdict: the requested successful and unsuccessful nonzero local cues are demonstrated in the same prepared computational cell.** Circumference, grid, cue position, support, peak and mixed boundary operator are held fixed. The 50-s pulse raises challenger Rac from 0.62425 to 3.31141, approximately 5.3-fold; its shared face trace exceeds the incumbent from the sampled 3.5-s point through 175 s. Nevertheless, after 13000 s without the cue, incumbent/challenger Rac is 0.89557/0.62197. The 1000-s reference instead ends at 0.62197/0.89557 after the same cue-free duration. The endpoint fields each contain one connected periodic half-contrast-high domain, located near the incumbent or challenger respectively. Thus the failed example has substantial transient local activation, rather than an ineffective cue.

The same-state claim is unusually well supported. The full-loop and controller checkpoint files are byte-identical between campaigns. Direct comparison of all eleven fields on both arcs gives exactly zero error at every native saved sample through each withdrawal: 5, 21 and 101 samples for 2, 10 and 50 s. The frozen worker uses one uninterrupted update loop; withdrawal only switches the external cue to zero before subsequent updates. There is no withdrawal reinitialization or projection. The unchanged-prefix result therefore reflects a genuine fork of the same evolving state. My saved-field total-MCA check agrees with the reported maximum error of 2.67 × 10⁻¹⁵; the linked audit additionally closes native interval and cumulative per-arc budgets, with the largest cumulative error 6.76 × 10⁻¹¹. Accounting validates implementation consistency, not a biological transport mechanism.

**The interpretation should remain bounded as written.** Both mechanical cuts are prescribed and retained; this is not the newly proposed Rac-threshold gap-opening/closure rule. Shared Rac/Rho/unbound-MCA diffusive traces coexist with separate mechanics, tension and bound MCA, native donor-cell unbound advection and global replacement. Retention over the observed recovery is supported; proven attractor stability, a commitment variable and physical turning are not established. Duration also changes cumulative cue exposure. Disclosing all three prespecified short failures and choosing the longest unsuccessful one is transparent illustration, without identifying a switching threshold or a duration law. No additional campaign is required to satisfy this concrete demonstration.

**Anticipated user critique:** “The short cue makes Rac very high, yet the cell returns. Does that mean the gap closes or the challenger never commits?” The supported answer is that strong cue-driven Rac activation can reverse after withdrawal while the prescribed mechanical gap remains open. The longer pulse reaches a different retained coupled state; these two histories do not identify a commitment criterion. The separate Rac-controlled gap proposal remains an untested model change.
