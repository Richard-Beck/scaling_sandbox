Define maximum excursion as maximum angular deviation from the same pre-event lab-frame heading, and state the angular-difference convention. A maximum measured before observation ends is a within-window maximum; it is not necessarily the maximum of an ongoing or incompletely observed event. These are definitions of the proposed readouts, not additional measurements.
