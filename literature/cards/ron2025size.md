# Ron & Adar 2025: larger contact area makes polarization easier

**Why it matters to us.** A different mechanism by which larger cells could migrate more persistently: easier front–rear gradients across a larger contact area.

## What they did
Minimal theory linking cell size to membrane potential and actin-driven forces, with cell volume and surface area as dynamic variables. Motivated by experiments in which cells lose volume as they spread and swell when activated, and migration speed tracks size.

## Results
- [R] Polarization arises from a gradient of a polarity cue along the contact ring, and is more easily established across larger domains. This gives a critical contact area above which migration is possible.
- [R] In the HTML version, swollen cells have larger contact areas, polarize more, and migrate faster and more persistently.
- [R] The model is said to account for recent neutrophil experiments.

## What transfers
Size can raise persistence through contact geometry and force balance, without any change in domain competition.

## Relation to our claims
- Competing or complementary mechanism; cite in the discussion.
- (I) Their mechanism predicts speed rising with size. In Xuan and Mallin, enlarged cells are slower, so speed and persistence move apart. Our mechanism does not tie persistence to speed.
