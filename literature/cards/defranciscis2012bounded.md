# de Franciscis & d'Onofrio 2012: extrinsic noise can destroy wave-pinning polarity

**Why it matters to us.** Earlier study of extrinsic, spatio-temporally correlated noise in wave-pinning, our noise class.

## What they did
Numerical simulations of the Mori wave-pinning model with parameters perturbed by bounded noises that have spatial and temporal correlation.

## Results
- [R] Such perturbations can sometimes destroy the polarized state. Whether they do depends on the spatial and temporal correlation and on the noise model.
- [R] Spatially homogeneous noise destroyed polarization in most cases. More spatial correlation increased the probability of polarization. The effect of more temporal correlation depended on the noise model.

## What transfers
Polarity loss under extrinsic noise depends on noise correlation structure, not only amplitude.
